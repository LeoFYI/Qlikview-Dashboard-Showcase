**Demo Reports (Sample Data)**

**1. KPI Dashboard**: Provides an overview of key call centre performance metrics, including sales vs target, AHT, NPS and performance trends.

**2. Customer Survey Dashboard**: Tracks customer survey scores and feedback at the individual interaction level.

**3. Coaching Suite**: Combines customer interactions with related activities, including orders, enquiries and complaints, alongside survey and QA scores, with access to evaluation forms and call recordings.

**4. Call Activities Tracker**: Tracks activities associated with customer calls, including orders, payments, enquiries, refunds, claims and new customer registrations, with access to call recordings.

**5. Productivity Dashboard**: Tracks team productivity across calls, emails, chats and ancillary tasks.

**6. Enquiries & Complaints Suite**: Tracks customer enquiries and complaints, providing insights into volumes, trends, resolution status and service performance.

**7. Agent Productivity Tracker**: Tracks individual agent productivity across calls, emails, chats and ancillary tasks.

**8. Zendesk Performance Dashboard**: Tracks Zendesk performance, including chat volumes, message counts, CSAT, customer ratings and reply times, with quartile-based performance analysis.
